# Privacy Policy for Rephrase It

**Last updated:** January 25, 2026

## Overview

Rephrase It is a Chrome extension that helps users rephrase selected text using AI. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection

### What we access:
- **Selected text**: When you right-click and choose "Rephrase It", the extension reads the text you've selected on the webpage.

### What we store locally (in your browser):
- **API Key**: Your Google Gemini API key is stored locally in your browser's storage. It never leaves your device except to authenticate with Google's API.
- **History**: The last 2 rephrase pairs (original + rephrased text) are stored locally for your convenience.

### What we send externally:
- **Selected text**: When you request a rephrase, your selected text is sent to Google's Gemini API for processing.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect personal information (name, email, etc.)
- We do not track your browsing history
- We do not use analytics or tracking scripts
- We do not have servers that store your data
- We do not sell or share any data with third parties

## Third-Party Services

This extension uses **Google's Gemini API** to process text. When you rephrase text, it is sent to Google's servers. Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for information on how Google handles data.

## Data Security

- All API communications use HTTPS encryption
- Your API key is stored locally and only used to authenticate with Google
- No data is transmitted to any server other than Google's Gemini API

## Your Control

- You can delete your stored API key at any time through the extension popup
- You can clear your local history by clearing browser extension data
- You choose when to send text for rephrasing — nothing is sent automatically

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
